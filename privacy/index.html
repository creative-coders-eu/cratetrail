Privacy policy

Effective October 30, 2026.

Your inventory stays yours

CrateTrail stores box names, locations, contents, notes and selected photos locally on your iPhone. We do not collect, receive, sell or track this information. There are no accounts, advertising, analytics, third-party SDKs or CrateTrail servers.

Photos and camera

The system photo picker shares only images you choose. Photos are resized and saved locally. Camera access is optional and used only to scan QR labels; camera frames are not saved or transmitted. A QR contains a random box identifier, not its contents or location. The exported label also shows the box title and number. Share it only with people you choose.

Purchases and device backups

Apple processes purchases and provides purchase status through StoreKit. We never receive payment details. Your inventory may be included in your device backups under your Apple settings. There is no inventory sync between devices.

Control and deletion

Edit or delete records in the app, or erase all inventory in Settings. Deleting the app deletes local data. Manage device backups through Apple. We cannot recover inventory from our servers because we do not hold it.

Support and this website

If you email us, we receive the details you choose to send and use them to answer your request. Avoid sending sensitive inventory information. This website has no analytics or cookies added by us; GitHub Pages may process connection logs under its own privacy policy.

For privacy requests, contact admin@ccoders.eu.
