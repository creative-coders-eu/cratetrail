Support & FAQ

A little help getting everything home.

How do I start?

Tap Add box, choose a location, and add contents. Use Settings → Manage locations to add or rename places. The box number stays stable even when you move it.

How do QR labels work?

Open a box → Box QR label → Share label image. Print or save the PNG. Use Find → Scan a box label. Labels open the matching box on the iPhone with that inventory. They do not transfer contents to another phone.

Why is a label unknown?

The box may have been deleted, or the label may belong to a different phone. Search by box name or number. Enable camera access in iPhone Settings if scanning is unavailable.

Can I use it offline?

Yes. Inventory, photos, searching, QR labels and moving work offline. Purchasing and restoring Pro need the App Store. There is no cloud sync. Keep an iPhone backup before replacing your device.

How do I restore Pro?

Use the Apple Account that purchased it, then tap Restore Purchases in Settings. It is a one-time non-consumable purchase, not a subscription. Existing inventory stays accessible if your entitlement changes.

Need more help?

Email admin@ccoders.eu with your iOS version and what happened. Please do not include private contents or photos unless needed.
